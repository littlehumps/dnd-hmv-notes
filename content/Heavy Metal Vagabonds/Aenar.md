### Appearance: 
Aenar's first appearance was as a Fallen Aasimar with dark black hair pulled into one long braid. She has purple eyes and a strong frame. She carries a Knight's sword created from [[Innocence]] called [[../Lore/Ascalon]].

![[../attachments/Aenar-20250306200832750.webp]]

Following her ritual in the [[../Fire Plain/Fire Plain|Fire Plain]], Aenar emerged from the flames with a new appearance. Hair floating free, embellished with gold and red rings and large black wings with embers at the tips.

![[../attachments/Aenar-20250306175852827.webp]]
### Joining the Party
Aenar is a [[Champion of Tara]] and fought in the [[Great Calamity]]. She is currently in a new timeline and part of the [[Heavy Metal Vagabonds]]. 

The party met her in a cave north of [[Glima]], captured by Ogres. She was very confused about the year and where she was. During her time in the cave Aenar took a love potion by accident and had a very shot romantic relationship with [[Cherlodyte]], a troglodyte they were fighting. Cherlodyte was killed within minutes by another troglodyte and Aenar, devasted by her grief, left the party. 

### Journey to Shamuti
Aenar reappeared in [[Yar Harbour]], bumping into [[Wren]]. For a very brief moment she had a northern accent from her travels. She advised that [[Swamp Potato]] was in danger and that they must travel to [[Shamuti Island]] to save her. Wren and Aenar travelled to the island via magic after visiting [[Fumblemore]] in his lighthouse.

Aenar told the party about [[Jaster]] and his family and the dangers that may be ahead of them. For a while [[Aenar]] called people sluts because she didn't know what it meant. 

### Journey to the Fire Plains
In the swamp of Perolus the party met [[Key]], a mechanical bird. Key appeared to know Aenar from before the [[../Lore/Great Calamity|Great Calamity]], yet Aenar had no recollection of [[Key]]. Key then guided Aenar to a statue of [[Ramiel]], the 9th [[../Champions of Tara/Champion of Tara|Champion of Tara]], beneath a waterfall. 

Ellie, and eventually [[../Heavy Metal Vagabonds/Aenar|Aenar]], bring Ramiel back to his body by entering his mind, but it begins to crumble and his last words to Aenar share that what they were doing was wrong... they were wrong. At the same time [[Wren]] spoke to [[../Jaster's Family/The Judge|The Judge]] who showed her visions of her final moments before she died. For Wren a fog also cleared, showing that she was not shot in the eye with an arrow, but pierced by [[Aenar]]'s sword. 

[[Wren]] tried to lie to the party about her conversations with [[[../Jaster's Family/The Judge|The Judge]], but was soon caught in her deception, her anger targeted at [[Aenar]]. In [[Howl]]'s house, [[Aenar]] finds a book and starts to learn what noises different animals make.

In the [[../Fire Plain/Fire Plain|Fire Plain]], Aenar uses one of [[../Other NPCs/Howl|Howl]]'s collars to disguise herself as a slaver, with [[Wren]] as her slave. Her appearance is intimidating to many due to the rank of citizen she is seen as. Aenar witnesses an orb being sold during an auction at [[../Fire Plain/Hammer Time]] that appears to contain innocence. She questions the auction master but in doing, alerts suspicion and ravens begin to chase the party. 

To evade the chase, Aenar created two methods of distraction. She first casted darkness on a sausage, which she gave to [[Swamp Potato]] in her hellhound form. Swampy ran around the town with the [[Sausage of Darkness]], causing chaos but also shielding the party from view. Aenar also used the remainder of her love potion on a guard called [[../Fire Plain/Ank|Ank]], who was immediately enthralled by her beauty. She convinced Ank to hand all of his clothes to [[Ellie Smith]] to further conceal her appearance, along with all of his savings and his bag of holding - but not before begrudgingly giving him a kiss. 

When the ravens finally caught up with the party, one was revealed to be [[../Fire Plain/Evangelic|Evangelic]] the 14th [[../Champions of Tara/Champion of Tara|Champion of Tara]]. After a short battle in the square, Evangelic brough the party to her employer, [[../Fire Plain/Astaroph|Astaroph]], one of the lords of the Fire Plain.
### Joining Astaroph's Vanguard
Evangelic shared with Aenar her views on [[../Champions of Tara/Tara|Tara]], and why she chose to break free from her rule and join Astaroph and encouraged Aenar to do the same. Aenar broke down in front of [[Wren]], begging for her forgiveness after seeing the truth of Tara's manipulation and the destruction she herself had caused all those years ago. 

Before Aenar made a decision on her future, the party relaxed in the spa, and [[Syntak]] experienced a malfunction causing him to shut down. Aenar and [[Swamp Potato]] channelled their power together to try and connect with Syntak through a network of those who held innocence. After they successfully brought him back, Aenar glimpsed [[../Lore/Ascalon]] for the first time outside the spa. 

Astaroph convinced Aenar to join him, and be rid of Tara's control. She was to undertake a trial of fire, by walking into a pool of lava and facing what it brings. As she stepped through the flames, Tara tested her faith and resilience, but Aenar broke the chains that bonded them. She re-emerged with a new appearance, and a stronger connection with Ascalon. 

When [[../Fire Plain/Avianna Hardscrable|Avianna Hardscrable]] inquired about the disturbance in the city, Asteroph tasks Aenar and the party to find the culprit. Aenar interrogated the auction house owner whilst Wren stole his book of transactions and the party discover a man named [[../Snowcoat/Craven]] has been transporting slaves and artifacts to the Fire Plain. 

Aenar is suspicious of [[../Other NPCs/Howl|Howl]]'s involvement with Craven, and confides in Evangelic. Evangelic took her to Asteroph's treasury, and showed her an egg-like object infused with corrupt innocence and was concerned there may be more objects like this. 

The party devised a plan to capture Craven at the [[../Fire Plain/Jewelled Fox]], with [[Arra]] and [[Syntak]] posing as workers. After they successfully seduced Craven, Aenar helped to restrain him so that Syntak could contain him in an [[../Iron Flask]]. When the party opened the other flasks in Craven's possession, they discovered the one they had captured was actually one of the [[../Lore/Shapeshifters]] and the real Craven was now in front of them. 

Before the party were ready to share their evidence at the trial with Avianna, Aenar sensed more corrupt innocence and followed it to Asteroph's library where she found [[../Other NPCs/Howl|Howl]]. He appeared to be using the shards as a sort of drug, and vanished from the fireplace. 

At the trial, Aenar joined [[Syntak]] to take the stand and put forward a case that the Fire Plane was in danger of invasion from [[../Lore/Shapeshifters]]. Avianna revealed her involvement, transformed into her ancient dragon form and a battle began, with Howl taken on a new monstrous form due to the corrupted shards. 

[[../Fire Plain/Astaroph|Astaroph]] called for Aenar and the party to flee, throwing the keys that the [[../Lore/Shapeshifters]] had used to travel across plains to her. As the party fled through the new door to [[../Snowcoat/Snowcoat|Snowcoat]], Aenar looked back at [[../Fire Plain/Evangelic|Evangelic]] and Asteroph who had given her a new purpose in life, locked the door behind her friends and re-joined the fight. Her final words in this moment were to Wren, "I hope you consider my debt re-payed", leaving the party unaware of her fate.
